# Mechanical drawing and schematic standards for VizCraft notation packs

Researched 2026-10-19. Paid texts were not read; facts come from catalogue pages, free previews, RTI-published national adoptions and secondary sources, each linked. "(secondary)" marks values only a secondary source supports; "(unverified)" marks unconfirmed editions. The search budget ran out before ISO 16016, the 2025 ISO 14405-1, Y14.3 content, Simulink and QCAD templates could be checked.

Licensing: ASME, ISO, IEC and AWS texts and figures are copyrighted. The Indian adoptions on archive.org and law.resource.org are readable copies of ISO 3952, 5457 and 7200; BIS says its copyright "does not preclude the free use, in the course of implementing the standard, of necessary details, such as symbols and sizes" ([IS 10711:2001](https://archive.org/download/gov.in.is.10711.2001/is.10711.2001.pdf)). Use them as descriptions to draw from, never as artwork to trace.

## 5. Sheet frames and title blocks as a feature

ISO 5457 fixes the geometry (section 2) and ISO 7200 the fields; block size is open. ASME Y14.1 fixes sizes, zones and the lower-right title block; Y14.35 reuses its revision block and Y14.100 requires the application block (NEXT ASSY, USED ON) for items of a larger item. Y14.1 margins and zone lettering direction were not found free; the public-domain NASA GSFC manual and MIL-STD-100G are the practical references (section 7). Tools model a sheet template as an SVG page with frame, grid border and a title block whose text fields are keyed by name and filled from document properties, as FreeCAD's ASME and ISO folders do ([TechDraw Templates](https://wiki.freecad.org/TechDraw_Templates), [folder](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/tree/main/src/Mod/TechDraw/Templates)). Balloon practice is company-level, so the tool may own it.

## Implications for notation packs (synthesis)

Marked as synthesis: my design derived from the above, not a standard's text.

**Sheet and Title Block pack.** Types: `Sheet` (standard ISO5457 | ASMEY14.1, size, orientation, units), `Frame` (border widths, frame width, grid field size and counts, letter direction, excluded letters), `CentringMark`, `TrimmingMark`, `TitleBlock`, `RevisionTable`, `PartsList`, `ApplicationBlock`, `ProjectionSymbol` (first | third), `ToleranceBlock`, `ProtectionNotice`. Title block fields bound to document properties under ISO 7200 names and limits (legal_owner, identification_number, revision_index, date_of_issue, sheet_number, sheet_count, title, approval_person, creator, document_type enum from ISO 29845 or Y14.24, plus optional fields) and ASME extras (drawing_number, cage_code, scale, material, finish, weight, drawn/checked/approved dates). Identifiers: sheets 1..m; revision index from a pluggable skip-set sequence; item numbers 1..n. `Balloon` has one leader and a row reference; `RevisionSymbol` a letter, optional sequence number and a zone computed from position. Validations: section 6 rows for sheet, revision table, assembly.

**Mechanical Schematic pack.** Types from ISO 3952: `Link` (number, ground flag), `Joint` (kind, dof), `Shaft`, `Bearing`, `Coupling` (kind), `Clutch` (kind; actuation M H P E), `Brake`, `Gear` (kind; teeth), `FrictionWheel`, `Cam`, `Follower`, `Geneva`, `Ratchet`, `Pulley`, `Belt`, `Sprocket`, `Chain`, `Motor`, `Load`; bond graph subfamily Se, Sf, R, C, I, TF, GY, J0, J1 with a `Bond` connector carrying power direction and stroke end. Ports: joints two link ports; shafts an ordered mount list; gears one shaft and one mesh port; one-ports one, TF and GY two, junctions n. Connections: Link-Joint-Link; Gear-Gear mesh; Shaft mounts Gear | Pulley | Coupling | Clutch | Brake | Bearing; Belt Pulley-Pulley; Chain Sprocket-Sprocket; Bond any-any under junction constraints. Identifiers: links 1..n, points A.., shafts S1.., bonds numbered. Validations: mobility shown; bond causality; ratio along a gear path.

**Control Block Diagram pack.** Types: `TransferBlock` (G(s)), `Gain`, `Integrator`, `SummingJunction` (signed inputs), `BranchPoint`, `Input`, `Output`, `Saturation`, `Delay`; `Signal` connector with label; signal-flow view with `Node` and gain `Branch`. Ports: blocks one in one out; summing n signed in, one out; branch one in, n out. Validations: no dangling ports, signed inputs, loops listed for Mason, algebraic-loop warning.

**Reliability pack.** Types: FTA events (top, intermediate, basic, house, undeveloped, conditioning), gates AND, OR, XOR, PAND, Inhibit, Voting(k,n), TransferIn, TransferOut; RBD `Start`, `End`, `Block` (R or lambda), `Voting`, `Standby`; `FmeaTable` with IEC 60812 columns; ETA `Initiator`, `Branch`, `Outcome`; bow-tie `Hazard`, `TopEvent`, `Threat`, `Consequence`, `Barrier`, `EscalationFactor`; Ishikawa `Effect`, `Category`, `Cause`. Ports: gates one out, n in; events one out; RBD blocks in and out. Identifiers: E1.., G1.., transfers by label. Validations: section 4 rules.

**Annotation pack.** Types: `FeatureControlFrame` (characteristic enum of 14 with per-standard availability; tolerance; diameter flag; modifiers M L P F T U; up to three datum references; composite rows), `DatumFeatureSymbol`, `DatumTarget`, `WeldingSymbol` (convention ISO2553 | AWSA2.4; arrow-side and other-side symbols; size, length, pitch; all-around, site, contour, backing; tail text and ISO 4063 number), `SurfaceTexture` (ISO21920 | Y14.36; variant; parameter, value, lay, process), `RevisionSymbol`, `Balloon`, `SectionLine`, `ViewLabel` (letters, scale), `Note` with Y14.38 lint. Ports: one leader each; datum symbol attaches to a feature line or an FCF. Identifiers: datums and section letters A.. skipping I O Q (secondary practice). Connections: FCF datum references resolve to datum symbols on the same drawing; a welding arrow lands on a joint line; texture attaches to a surface or leader. Validations: section 6 rows for detail drawing, welding, texture.
